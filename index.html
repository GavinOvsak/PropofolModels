Manual boluses

 

Tap the chart to pick a time, or tap a logged bolus to edit it. Each bolus lowers the suggested rate for a few minutes, and a large one can pause it, so the effect site comes back to target. Times must be after the induction start.

For educational use only, not for clinical use. This tool is not a medical device and does not replace clinical judgment, your pump, or your institution's protocols. This browser blocked saving, so a refresh will clear the case.

Predicted BIS comes from the Eleveld model only. Treat gaps between predicted and entered BIS as a rough guide, since BIS also moves with stimulation, other drugs and artifact.

How rates are worked out

Rates hold the target effect-site concentration once it has been reached, and are rounded to the nearest 10 mcg/kg/min.

The initial bolus is ignored, and so are any boluses given for target changes. Boluses you log in the Manual boluses section are included: each one lowers the suggested rate for a few minutes, and a large one can pause it.

Suggested rates go down over time as drug builds up in the tissues.
